# Business Insights & Recommendations

## 1. Overall Business Performance

- The business generated ₹117.72M in total revenue from 9,992 orders.
- The customer base consists of 3,851 unique customers.
- Customers purchased a total of 24,085 units.
- The average order value was approximately ₹11.78K.
- Returning customers represent 67.9% of the customer base, indicating relatively strong repeat-purchase behavior.
- However, 32.1% of customers are one-time buyers, creating a significant opportunity for customer retention and repeat-purchase campaigns.

- ## 2. Category Performance

- Electronics is the dominant revenue-generating category, contributing approximately 70% of total revenue.
- Home & Kitchen is the second-largest category by revenue.
- Books and Beauty & Personal Care contribute comparatively smaller portions of total revenue.
- The strong dependence on Electronics represents both a major revenue strength and a potential concentration risk.
   
- ### Recommendation

The company should continue investing in high-performing Electronics products while developing strategies to increase cross-selling and upselling in lower-performing categories.

Diversifying revenue across categories could reduce dependence on a single category and improve overall business resilience.

## 3. Customer Value Segmentation

- High-value customers represent approximately 25% of the customer base.
- Despite their relatively small share of customers, they contribute approximately 69.5% of total revenue.
- Their average revenue per customer is approximately ₹85K.
- Low-value customers represent approximately 25% of customers but contribute only about 2.5% of revenue.

- ### Recommendation

The company should prioritize retention of high-value customers through loyalty programs, personalized offers, early access to products, and targeted engagement.

At the same time, low-value customers should be targeted with strategies designed to increase purchase frequency and average order value.
